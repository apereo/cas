You are viewing the development documentation for the Apereo CAS server. The functionality presented here is not officially released yet. This is a work in progress and will be continually updated as development moves forward. You are most encouraged to test the changes presented.

Account Unlock - Password Management

In the event that the authentication source has determine the account status to either be locked or disabled, the password management facility in CAS is able to intercept that flow automatically and allow the user to unlock and enable their account. The account unlock flow is fairly simple and modest and provides a reCAPTCHA-like unlocking mechanism. Additional modifications and steps in this flow will require further customizations. The unlocking of the account is ultimately passed onto the account storage service and depends on whether the storage service (JSON, LDAP, etc) supports this capability.
