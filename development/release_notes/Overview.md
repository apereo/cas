- ## [8.1.0-RC3](https://apereo.github.io/cas/development/release_notes/RC3.html)

  The third release candidate reworks Heimdall around the AuthZEN 1.0 specification, brings WebAuthn Level 3 and passkeys to passwordless and multifactor login, and carries the stateless ticket-granting ticket in its cookie.

  Heimdall AuthZEN · WebAuthn Level 3 · Passwordless Authentication · Stateless Ticket Registry · OpenID Connect Verifiable Credentials · Documentation

  **14**topics **9**to review **129**other changes
- ## [8.1.0-RC2](https://apereo.github.io/cas/development/release_notes/RC2.html)

  The second release candidate hardens OAuth, OpenID Connect, SAML2 and the CAS protocol across many flows, moves verifiable credentials to the final OpenID4VCI 1.0 format, and fixes how consent decisions are stored and revoked.

  OAuth & OpenID Connect Hardening · SAML2 Identity Provider · OpenID Connect Verifiable Credentials · CAS Protocol · Attribute Consent · JMX Management

  **21**topics **6**to review **5**other changes
- ## [8.1.0-RC1](https://apereo.github.io/cas/development/release_notes/RC1.html)

  The first release candidate of the 8.1.0 line turns Palantir into a place to manage authorization policies, attribute repositories and cluster topology, and lets OAuth and OpenID Connect applications rotate multiple client secrets.

  Multiple Client Secrets · Cluster Topology · Attribute Definition Dependencies · OpenID Connect Verifiable Credentials · OpenID Connect Federation · Acceptable Usage Policy & Multitenancy
